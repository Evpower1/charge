Privacy at Evpower.

Evpower uses your information to arrange charging, manage your account and service credit, and help when you need support. This notice covers the Evpower mobile app and its connected service.

Updated 10 November 2026 · Evpower · Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Location is your choice.

You can enter a pickup address yourself. The app asks for foreground location only when you choose to use your current position.

Payment checkout stays with Chapa.

When payment is available, Chapa handles checkout. The Evpower app does not collect or store your card number, payment PIN or mobile-money password.

Location and permissions

The app does not request background location. You may deny location access or switch it off in your device settings and still enter an address manually. Opening directions or a third-party map sends the selected place to that map provider. Map providers handle information under their own terms and privacy notices.

App preferences and security features can depend on your device. Native sign-in uses the device’s secure storage for its refresh credential. Passwords are stored on the service as salted hashes rather than readable passwords.

Payments and other providers

If you start a service-credit top-up, Evpower sends Chapa the amount, currency, transaction reference and the account name and email needed for checkout. Evpower verifies the result with the payment provider before crediting the balance. Chapa and the payment method you select may process additional information in their own checkout.

Evpower’s authorized service team can access the details needed to manage requests and support. Hosting, email and payment providers process the information needed to operate their part of the service. Calling, emailing or opening WhatsApp from the app uses the selected external service.

The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs in this release.

Keeping and removing information

Your account and saved details are kept while you use the service. You can edit your profile and remove saved vehicles or addresses in Account.

You can request account deletion in the app or through the contact method on our account-deletion page. Deletion revokes access, removes saved vehicle and address details and removes identifying profile details. Transaction and audit records may be retained without direct profile details for financial reconciliation, fraud prevention and applicable record-keeping requirements.

An active service or remaining service-credit balance may need to be resolved with support before deletion can complete. We do not ask you to send your password or payment PIN to request help.

Your choices and questions

You can choose whether to share your current location, manage saved details in the app, sign out, or request deletion. For a copy of your information, a correction you cannot make yourself, or a privacy question, contact Evpower at info@evpower.et or +251 911 049 916. We may need to verify that a request comes from the account holder.

If the service’s data practices change, this notice will be updated with a new date.

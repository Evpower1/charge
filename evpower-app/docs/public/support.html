For a booking or payment question: include your booking or transaction reference and the email used for your account. Never send a password, card number, payment PIN or one-time sign-in code.

Charging requests

Submitting a request asks the Evpower team to arrange mobile charging. It does not guarantee immediate dispatch. Your request appears in Activity, where you can see its status, operator quote and updates.

Check the confirmed details before paying a quote. If the pickup address or connector is wrong, contact support before the service begins. Eligible cancellations before charging return paid booking credit to your Evpower service-credit balance.

Station availability

Station status depends on recent information from connected charger equipment. Unknown means Evpower does not have a current status for that connector. It is not a promise that a charger is available.

If you are travelling to a station and need confirmation, call the team. Only verified, configured stations are shown in the production app.

Service credit and payments

Evpower service credit is for Evpower charging bookings. This app does not provide transfers to other customers or cash withdrawals.

A top-up is credited only after payment verification. If a payment remains pending, use the payment-check option in the app or contact support with its reference. You do not need to make the same payment again to request help. For a payment-provider refund or an unresolved balance, speak with the team.

Account help

Use Forgot password from the sign-in screen if you cannot sign in. In Account, you can edit your profile, manage saved vehicles and addresses, see notifications and send a support request.

To remove an account, follow the account-deletion instructions. Our privacy notice explains how service information is used.

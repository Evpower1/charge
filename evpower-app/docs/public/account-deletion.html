You control your account

Delete your Evpower account.

You can delete your account from the Evpower app or contact the team to request deletion without reinstalling it.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in and open Account.
  2. Expand Delete account.
  3. Read what will be removed, enter your password and confirm that you understand the deletion is permanent.
  4. Select Delete my account permanently.

If you have remaining service credit or an in-progress charging service, contact support to resolve it before deletion can complete.

Request deletion without the app

Email info@evpower.et with the subject Evpower account deletion request. Tell us the email address registered to your account. Sending the request from that address helps us verify ownership.

You can also call +251 911 049 916 or 8890 toll-free in Ethiopia for help. We may ask you to complete an account-ownership check. Do not send your password, payment PIN or full payment details.

Request account deletion

What deletion removes

Access sessions are revoked. Your identifying profile details, saved vehicles and saved addresses are removed. You will no longer be able to sign in to the deleted account.

Records that may remain

Transaction and audit records may be retained without direct profile details for financial reconciliation, fraud prevention and applicable record-keeping requirements.

Deleting the app from your phone does not itself delete your Evpower account. Use the in-app action or contact method above. For questions about retained records, email info@evpower.et.

Need something else?

If you only want to update a phone number or remove a saved address, you can do that in Account without deleting the account. Read our privacy notice or contact support for more help.
